Privacy Policy · Epoch Arena

Last updated: October 30, 2026 Provider: Young King

This policy explains information handling in the current iPhone and iPad version of Epoch Arena.

1. Progress and preferences No game account is required. Hero unlocks, mission progress, the in-game display name, tower records and preferences are stored on your device. The current game does not provide developer-operated cloud saves or account synchronization.

2. Advertising and automatic reporting The current version does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, advertising-identifier use, or an automatic analytics or crash-upload service. We do not receive your gameplay activity or device data through such services.

3. Local multiplayer When you choose LAN Match, devices discover rooms on the local network and directly exchange display names, selected heroes, local connection information and gameplay data with participants in the same room. This traffic does not pass through a developer-operated game server. Other participants can see your display name and in-game actions. Single-player play does not require Local Network permission.

4. App Store purchases Purchases and restoration are handled by Apple StoreKit under Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. The game reads purchase entitlements on the device to determine content access. The developer does not thereby receive your payment-card number, billing address or Apple Account password. Apple's store services may require an internet connection.

5. Local diagnostics and voluntary contact Limited technical logs are stored on the device for troubleshooting. Copy diagnostics in Settings copies a report to the clipboard and does not automatically send it. If you voluntarily email support and attach diagnostic information, we use the contact details, issue description and attachments you provide to address that request. Please share only information relevant to the issue.

6. Managing and deleting information You can manage Local Network permission in system settings. Deleting the app removes its local game data; data retained in system backups is managed by the relevant system service. Restore purchase restores eligible purchase entitlements and does not automatically migrate game saves. For questions about information you voluntarily sent to support, use the email below.

7. Age suitability The game contains frequent fantasy combat and weapons. Refer to its App Store age rating. The current version does not include child-directed advertising, tracking or game-account registration.

8. Changes If information handling changes, we will update this policy and its date and describe the change where appropriate.

9. Contact Privacy and support email: shadeless@126.com
