Support · Epoch Arena

Provider: Young King Support email: shadeless@126.com

CONTACT SUPPORT Email us with your device model, OS version, game version and steps to reproduce the issue. You may voluntarily include the report from Settings > Copy diagnostics. Do not include your Apple Account password or payment-card details.

WHAT IS FREE? All four Chapter 1 missions, four starter heroes and the first ten tower floors are free to try. LAN Match does not require the full-game purchase.

WHAT DOES THE FULL UNLOCK INCLUDE? A one-time purchase permits Chapters 2–5, all 39 hero acquisition paths and the tower without a floor cap. Heroes and missions still follow their acquisition and progression requirements. There is no subscription or consumable purchase. Local App Store pricing applies.

HOW DO I BUY OR RESTORE? From the main menu, open Settings > Full Version. The sheet includes the purchase button and Restore purchase. Restore with the Apple Account used for the original purchase and an active App Store connection.

DO I GET EVERY HERO IMMEDIATELY? No. The purchase opens every hero's acquisition path. Individual heroes still require their normal progression or unlock conditions.

CAN I PLAY OFFLINE? Single-player play needs no game account and works offline. Purchases and restoration need an App Store connection. Multiplayer requires all devices to be on the same local network.

WHY CAN'T I FIND MY FRIEND'S ROOM? Connect all devices to the same Wi-Fi and allow the game's Local Network permission. Avoid guest networks that isolate devices. You can also join with the host's invite code. Internet matchmaking is not supported.

DOES RESTORE PURCHASE RESTORE MY SAVE? No. It restores eligible purchase entitlements. The current game has no developer-operated cloud save. Save migration depends on your system backup or device-transfer method; restoring a purchase cannot recover unbacked-up local progress.

HOW DO I CHANGE LANGUAGE? Choose Chinese or English in the language options near the top of Settings.

REFUNDS Apple handles App Store transactions. Use Apple's purchase-history and problem-reporting channels for information about refund requests.
